## Context note

Section 73 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872 deals with the right to recover
compensation for losses arising directly and naturally from a breach of
contract, as well as losses that the parties knew at the time of contracting
were likely to result from the breach.

In a freelance payment dispute, this section is commonly referenced when a
client fails to pay and the freelancer seeks to recover the invoice amount or
additional losses caused by the non-payment.

**Important limitation:** This note is for contextual reference only.
Whether Section 73 applies to a particular payment clause and whether
compensation is recoverable depends on specific facts and is a matter for
a qualified lawyer. Do not infer enforceability from this reference alone.
