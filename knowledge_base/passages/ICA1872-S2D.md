## Context note

Section 2(d) of the Indian Contract Act, 1872 defines "consideration" as the
element exchanged between contracting parties. When one party does or abstains
from doing something at the promisor's desire, that act or abstinence is called
the consideration for the promise.

Payment terms in a freelance contract establish the consideration owed to the
freelancer for services rendered.
