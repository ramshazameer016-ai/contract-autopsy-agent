## Context note

Section 19 of the Copyright Act, 1957 sets out the requirements for a valid
assignment of copyright. Among the key requirements is that an assignment of
copyright must be in writing and signed by the assignor or their authorised
agent.

Section 19 also addresses the duration and territorial extent of an assignment,
and provides protections for authors including reversion rights in certain
circumstances (added by the 2012 amendment).

In freelance contracts, IP assignment clauses that purport to transfer all
copyright to the client should be evaluated with these formal requirements in
mind. An assignment that does not meet the requirements of Section 19 may not
be legally effective.

**Important limitation:** This note is for contextual reference only.
Whether a particular IP clause in a contract constitutes a valid assignment
under Section 19, and what remedies are available, is a matter for a qualified
lawyer. Do not use this reference to conclude that any contract clause is
valid or invalid.
